# Simple robot enclosure — design brief

Status: no CAD/STL files or physical fit validation yet. Owner has access to 3D printing.

Design: one rounded box-shaped hollow head/body with integrated stubby feet and a removable rear cover. miniPiTFT face; webcam chest opening; independent camera aim toward monitor; face toward user. Pi and speaker remain outside initially. No articulated arms, servo, or antenna. Aim for open-back-down printing, but verify supports/bridging in the slicer after geometry exists.
